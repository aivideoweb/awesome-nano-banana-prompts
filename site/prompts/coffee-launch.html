English prompt

Use case: Coffee launch poster. Target aspect ratio: 4:5.
An iced orange coffee in a clear glass with fine condensation against coral orange. A curled orange peel leads the eye; a few distinct ice cubes. Large headline above, a single subtitle below; no prices or promotional claims.
Text requirement: Headline: ORANGE HOUR; subtitle: A brighter coffee break. No other lettering, watermark or signature.
Maintain clear visual hierarchy, useful negative space and consistent light direction. Do not add unrequested objects. Deliver one finished image.

中文提示词

用途：咖啡新品海报。目标画幅：4:5。
一杯冰橙咖啡在珊瑚橙背景前，透明玻璃杯凝有细小水珠，橙皮卷形成引导线，冰块数量适中；上方大标题，下方单行副标题，不添加价格或促销承诺。
文字要求：标题：ORANGE HOUR；副标题：A brighter coffee break。除指定文字外不添加任何文字、水印或签名。
构图保持清晰层级、合理留白和统一光源。不要添加未要求的物件。输出单张完成图。

Quality review

Check spelling, ice perspective and glass contact shadow.
