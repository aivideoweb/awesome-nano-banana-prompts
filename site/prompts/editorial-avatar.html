English prompt

Use case: Natural professional avatar. Target aspect ratio: 1:1.
Head-and-shoulders portrait of a fictional adult architect wearing a brown cotton shirt with a relaxed expression. Defocused light gray wall, sharp eyes, natural skin texture and asymmetry, soft window light.
Text requirement: No text. No other lettering, watermark or signature.
Maintain clear visual hierarchy, useful negative space and consistent light direction. Do not add unrequested objects. Deliver one finished image.
