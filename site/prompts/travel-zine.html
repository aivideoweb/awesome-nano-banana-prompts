English prompt

Use case: Travel zine spread. Target aspect ratio: 3:2.
Fictional coastal-town travel zine spread: harbor photo on left page, old blue doorway and bowl of citrus in two detail photos on right. Wide margins, fine black rules, minimal headline and visible center gutter.
Text requirement: A SLOWER COAST. No other lettering, watermark or signature.
Maintain clear visual hierarchy, useful negative space and consistent light direction. Do not add unrequested objects. Deliver one finished image.
