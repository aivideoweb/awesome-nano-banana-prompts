English prompt

Use case: Catalog: verifiable seam detail. Suggested aspect ratio: 1:1; set this in your client.
Image 1 is the authorized garment photo and image 2 is its actual cuff close-up. Use image 2 as factual reference for a close view of cuff, button and double topstitching. Soft grazing light reveals fibers without changing stitch spacing or buttonhole shape. Background is defocused matching fabric only. Do not invent illegible care-label copy or add marks.
Deliver one finished image per step. Run multi-round or series tasks separately rather than combining them.

中文提示词

用途：服装目录：可核对的缝线细节。建议画幅：1:1，请在客户端设置。
图1为获授权服装照片，图2为该服装真实袖口微距。以图2为事实依据，生成袖口、纽扣和双道明线的近景，柔和掠射光突出纤维，不改变线迹间距或扣眼形状。背景仅保留同一面料虚化区域。不得把参考图看不清的护理文字补写出来，不新增标识。
输出单张完成图；多轮或系列任务按步骤分别运行，不能自行合并。

Quality review

Check buttonhole, stitching and fabric against the reference; do not present invented detail as a real product photo.

扣孔、线迹和布料纹理必须有参考依据；不能把补绘细节当商品实拍。

How to iterate

Set aspect ratio and resolution in your image client. Start at 1K. Fix one failed criterion at a time while preserving everything else; restart from your original reference if identity drifts.
