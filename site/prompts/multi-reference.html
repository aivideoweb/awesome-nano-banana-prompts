English prompt

Use case: Three-reference product composite. Target aspect ratio: 16:9.
Image 1 defines the owned product identity; image 2 provides layout only; image 3 provides palette only. Place the product at the center of image 2’s table with image 3’s warm gray and teal lighting. Do not import brands or objects from images 2 or 3.
Text requirement: Keep product lettering from image 1; add none. No other lettering, watermark or signature.
Maintain clear visual hierarchy, useful negative space and consistent light direction. Do not add unrequested objects. Deliver one finished image.

中文提示词

用途：三图产品场景融合。目标画幅：16:9。
图1为自有产品的外观依据，图2只提供场景布局，图3只提供色调；将图1产品放到图2桌面的中央，用图3的暖灰与青绿配色重新打光，不混合图2或图3中的品牌与物体。
文字要求：保留图1产品文字，不新增文字。除指定文字外不添加任何文字、水印或签名。
构图保持清晰层级、合理留白和统一光源。不要添加未要求的物件。输出单张完成图。

Quality review

Verify identity, layout and palette separately; correct one drift at a time.

分别核对身份、布局和色调；一次只纠正一个偏差。

How to iterate

Set aspect ratio and resolution in your image client. Start at 1K. Fix one failed criterion at a time while preserving everything else; restart from your original reference if identity drifts.
