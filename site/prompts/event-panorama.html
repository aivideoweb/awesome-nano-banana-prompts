English prompt

Use case: Event panorama concept. Target aspect ratio: 4:1.
Ultrawide concept for a creator meetup: display boards left, round tables center, small speaking platform right. Continuous gray-white walls and warm light, no audience faces, aligned floor lines and open upper-center space for later type.
Text requirement: No text. No other lettering, watermark or signature.
Maintain clear visual hierarchy, useful negative space and consistent light direction. Do not add unrequested objects. Deliver one finished image.

中文提示词

用途：活动空间全景概念。目标画幅：4:1。
原创创作者交流活动的超宽空间概念，左侧展板、中部圆桌、右侧小型演讲台，统一灰白墙面与暖光；不出现观众面孔，地面线条连续，给后期标题留中央上方空间。
文字要求：无文字。除指定文字外不添加任何文字、水印或签名。
构图保持清晰层级、合理留白和统一光源。不要添加未要求的物件。输出单张完成图。
