English prompt

Use case: English-to-Spanish poster localization. Target aspect ratio: 4:5.
Reference image 1 is an owned English event poster. Replace only headline and button text; keep photo, logo, price, date, grid and colors unchanged. For longer Spanish wording reduce font size within that text box; never stretch glyphs.
Text requirement: Replace Create together with Crea en equipo; replace Join now with Únete ahora. No other lettering, watermark or signature.
Maintain clear visual hierarchy, useful negative space and consistent light direction. Do not add unrequested objects. Deliver one finished image.

中文提示词

用途：海报英文转西语。目标画幅：4:5。
参考图1为自有英文活动海报。只替换标题和按钮文字，主体照片、品牌标识、价格、日期、网格和颜色不变；西语稍长时仅缩小该文字框字号，不拉伸字形。
文字要求：将Create together替换为Crea en equipo；将Join now替换为Únete ahora。除指定文字外不添加任何文字、水印或签名。
构图保持清晰层级、合理留白和统一光源。不要添加未要求的物件。输出单张完成图。

Quality review

Check accents and unchanged copy, dates and prices.

校对重音字符和其他文字；不能改动日期或价格。

How to iterate

Set aspect ratio and resolution in your image client. Start at 1K. Fix one failed criterion at a time while preserving everything else; restart from your original reference if identity drifts.
