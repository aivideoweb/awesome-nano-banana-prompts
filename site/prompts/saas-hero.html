English prompt

Use case: Creative tool landing page concept. Target aspect ratio: 16:9.
Desktop landing-page concept for a fictional creative collaboration tool. Headline and one button left, three task cards right. White background, navy type, teal accent and generous spacing; avoid dense microcopy.
Text requirement: IDEAS INTO MOTION; Start a project; Plan; Create; Review. No other lettering, watermark or signature.
Maintain clear visual hierarchy, useful negative space and consistent light direction. Do not add unrequested objects. Deliver one finished image.

中文提示词

用途：创作工具首页概念。目标画幅：16:9。
虚构创意协作工具的桌面首页视觉稿，左侧标题和单个按钮，右侧为三张任务卡；白底、深蓝文字、青绿强调色，留白充足，避免密集微小文字。
文字要求：IDEAS INTO MOTION；Start a project；Plan；Create；Review。除指定文字外不添加任何文字、水印或签名。
构图保持清晰层级、合理留白和统一光源。不要添加未要求的物件。输出单张完成图。

Quality review

A visual concept only, not a functional website or clickable interface.
