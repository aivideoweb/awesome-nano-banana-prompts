English prompt

Use case: Exhibition poster copy and object revision. Suggested aspect ratio: 4:5; set this in your client.
Image 1 is an owned exhibition poster. Set headline to “GLASS STUDIES”, subtitle to “Light in everyday objects”, dates to “06.12 — 06.28”. Replace only the central ceramic bowl with a clear glass bowl of the same size and position under matching light. Preserve grid, type style, margins, color blocks, venue name and paper texture. Change no other copy.
Deliver one finished image per step. Run multi-round or series tasks separately rather than combining them.

中文提示词

用途：展览海报：文案与主物件联动修改。建议画幅：4:5，请在客户端设置。
图1为自有展览海报。把主标题改为“GLASS STUDIES”，副标题改为“Light in everyday objects”，日期改为“06.12 — 06.28”；只将中央陶碗换成同尺寸、同位置的透明玻璃碗，匹配原光线。网格、字体风格、页边距、装饰色块、场馆名和纸张纹理不变。除这三处文字外不改其他文字。
输出单张完成图；多轮或系列任务按步骤分别运行，不能自行合并。

Quality review

Proof the three text fields, then verify bowl placement and all unchanged information.

先校对三处文字，再核对玻璃碗的占位与未修改信息。

How to iterate

Set aspect ratio and resolution in your image client. Start at 1K. Fix one failed criterion at a time while preserving everything else; restart from your original reference if identity drifts.
