English prompt

Use case: Plant growth classroom diagram. Target aspect ratio: 3:2.
A four-stage classroom diagram from left to right: seed, sprout, seedling and flowering plant. Equal rounded panels, aligned soil levels, one-way connecting arrows, white background and gentle colors.
Text requirement: Seed; Sprout; Seedling; Flower. No other lettering, watermark or signature.
Maintain clear visual hierarchy, useful negative space and consistent light direction. Do not add unrequested objects. Deliver one finished image.

中文提示词

用途：植物生长课堂图。目标画幅：3:2。
儿童课堂用的四阶段植物生长图，从左到右为种子、萌芽、幼苗和开花；每个阶段独立置于相同大小的圆角框内，土壤线对齐，用单向箭头串联，白底柔和配色。
文字要求：Seed；Sprout；Seedling；Flower。除指定文字外不添加任何文字、水印或签名。
构图保持清晰层级、合理留白和统一光源。不要添加未要求的物件。输出单张完成图。
