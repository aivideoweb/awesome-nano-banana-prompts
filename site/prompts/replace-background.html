English prompt

Use case: Product background replacement. Target aspect ratio: 4:5.
Reference image 1 is the edit target. Replace only the background with a pale stone tabletop and warm white wall, adding a soft shadow at the contact point. Preserve product silhouette, color, logo, packaging text, camera angle and position.
Text requirement: Preserve existing lettering; add none. No other lettering, watermark or signature.
Maintain clear visual hierarchy, useful negative space and consistent light direction. Do not add unrequested objects. Deliver one finished image.

中文提示词

用途：产品背景替换。目标画幅：4:5。
参考图1为待编辑产品。只把背景替换成浅灰石材台面与暖白墙面，并根据产品接触点生成柔和投影；产品轮廓、颜色、商标、包装文字、镜头角度和画面位置全部保持。
文字要求：保留原图文字，不新增文字。除指定文字外不添加任何文字、水印或签名。
构图保持清晰层级、合理留白和统一光源。不要添加未要求的物件。输出单张完成图。
