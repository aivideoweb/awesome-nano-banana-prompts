English prompt

Use case: Ultrawide website banner. Target aspect ratio: 4:1.
Ultrawide still-life banner. Teal ceramic cup and rolled ivory paper in the left quarter, clean warm gray negative space across the central half, small yellow abstract sculpture in the right quarter. Continuous left-to-right light.
Text requirement: No text. No other lettering, watermark or signature.
Maintain clear visual hierarchy, useful negative space and consistent light direction. Do not add unrequested objects. Deliver one finished image.

中文提示词

用途：超宽网站横幅。目标画幅：4:1。
超宽静物横幅，左四分之一放一只青绿陶杯与卷起的米白纸，中间一半保留干净暖灰背景，右四分之一为小型黄色抽象雕塑；光线从左向右连续。
文字要求：无文字。除指定文字外不添加任何文字、水印或签名。
构图保持清晰层级、合理留白和统一光源。不要添加未要求的物件。输出单张完成图。

Quality review

Check seams, repeated objects and end crops; choose aspect ratio in tool settings.

检查接缝、重复物体与两端裁切；比例需在工具设置中选择。

How to iterate

Set aspect ratio and resolution in your image client. Start at 1K. Fix one failed criterion at a time while preserving everything else; restart from your original reference if identity drifts.
