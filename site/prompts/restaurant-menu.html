English prompt

Use case: Café digital menu concept. Target aspect ratio: 4:5.
Digital menu concept for a fictional small café. One drink photo right, café name above, three menu rows left. Dark brown and cream with dot separators; do not invent prices.
Text requirement: MELLOW CAFÉ; Espresso; Oat Latte; Cold Brew. No other lettering, watermark or signature.
Maintain clear visual hierarchy, useful negative space and consistent light direction. Do not add unrequested objects. Deliver one finished image.

中文提示词

用途：咖啡店数字菜单。目标画幅：4:5。
虚构小咖啡店数字菜单，一张饮品照片居右，上方为店名，左侧三行菜单；深棕与奶油白，圆点分隔，不添加未提供的价格。
文字要求：MELLOW CAFÉ；Espresso；Oat Latte；Cold Brew。除指定文字外不添加任何文字、水印或签名。
构图保持清晰层级、合理留白和统一光源。不要添加未要求的物件。输出单张完成图。
