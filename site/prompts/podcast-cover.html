English prompt

Use case: Podcast cover. Target aspect ratio: 1:1.
Abstract cream circular sound waves surround a terracotta sphere on a deep violet background, evoking a quiet evening conversation. Use two typography levels; the title should remain legible at phone-cover size.
Text requirement: AFTER HOURS; Small conversations, wider ideas. No other lettering, watermark or signature.
Maintain clear visual hierarchy, useful negative space and consistent light direction. Do not add unrequested objects. Deliver one finished image.

中文提示词

用途：播客封面。目标画幅：1:1。
抽象奶油色圆形声波围绕一颗赤陶色球体，在深紫背景上营造安静夜谈气氛；采用两级排版，缩小至手机封面仍能辨认标题。
文字要求：AFTER HOURS；Small conversations, wider ideas。除指定文字外不添加任何文字、水印或签名。
构图保持清晰层级、合理留白和统一光源。不要添加未要求的物件。输出单张完成图。
