Use case: Skincare kit flat lay. Target aspect ratio: 1:1.
Three fictional skincare containers: a frosted dropper bottle, white tube and low round jar. Arrange a loose triangle on apricot paper, with one clear gel drop at upper right and at least one cap-width of space between containers.
Text requirement: Only container labels: NOMA. No other lettering, watermark or signature.
Maintain clear visual hierarchy, useful negative space and consistent light direction. Do not add unrequested objects. Deliver one finished image.
