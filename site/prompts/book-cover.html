English prompt

Use case: Nonfiction book cover. Target aspect ratio: 2:3.
Fictional creative-method nonfiction cover. One cobalt line on cream paper changes from tangled knot to orderly loop. Title in upper half, author area blank, restrained design readable as a bookstore thumbnail.
Text requirement: MAKE ROOM FOR IDEAS. No other lettering, watermark or signature.
Maintain clear visual hierarchy, useful negative space and consistent light direction. Do not add unrequested objects. Deliver one finished image.

中文提示词

用途：非虚构书封面。目标画幅：2:3。
虚构创意方法书封面，奶油纸上用一条钴蓝线从乱结过渡到整齐圆环，标题在上半部，作者位置保持空白，设计克制且具备书店缩略图辨识度。
文字要求：MAKE ROOM FOR IDEAS。除指定文字外不添加任何文字、水印或签名。
构图保持清晰层级、合理留白和统一光源。不要添加未要求的物件。输出单张完成图。
