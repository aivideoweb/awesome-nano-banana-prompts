How do I use a Nano Banana 2.1 prompt?

Open an entry, copy your preferred language, replace the fictional subject with your own brief and select the aspect ratio in your image tool. Editing recipes require authorized reference images. Generate a small draft, check the listed criteria, then revise one issue at a time.

Can I use the library commercially?

The repository's original text and code use the MIT license. Generated images are not a guarantee of exclusive rights or clearance. Review any visible marks, people and third-party materials before publication.

Are these verified model outputs?

No. The target model is documented separately from the illustrative preview generator. Each entry records its status in the JSON catalog.
